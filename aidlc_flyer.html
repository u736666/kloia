Company-aware AI workflows for your delivery lifecycle.
AIDLC brings AI into your delivery process in a controlled, context-aware and measurable way. We design AI workflows around the real context of each project: your codebase, ADRs, coding standards, business rules and runbooks, across planning, modernization, development, QA, DevOps and operations.
How It Works
01
Enterprise Context Foundation
Confluence, source repos, ADRs, target architecture and past decisions, connected via read-only connectors, MCP where applicable.
02
AI Agents Embedded in the Pipeline
Agents fed with your company context sit at each phase. Each phase's output becomes the next phase's input.
03
Compliance Layer Audits Every Output
Every output is checked against ADRs, security policies and coding standards. Violations are blocked and cited automatically.
04
Meta-Loop Improves the System
The pipeline watches itself and reports weekly. Humans decide, the system updates: AI informing humans, not AI fixing AI.
The Lifecycle
Define → Refine → Build → Review → Ship → Operate → Learn
"Learn" feeds straight back into "Define"; every release makes the next one better informed, not just faster.
